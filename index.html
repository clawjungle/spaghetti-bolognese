Ingredients

Spaghetti, olive oil, onion, garlic, mince.

Tomato paste (1 sachet, ~7g), 1 tin whole Italian or chopped tomatoes.

1/2 stock cube, splash of red/white wine, optional celery + carrot.

2 tbsp dried oregano, fistful fresh basil leaves (or dried basil).

Method

Start pasta water first. Chop onion and garlic. Warm pan.

Cook onion to translucent, add garlic halfway, remove both to a plate.

Brown mince well (about 1/3 browned), drain excess fat if needed.

Add everything back in, keep at gentle bubble, stir while simmering.

Salt pasta water to ~1% (about 15 to 20g for 1.5 to 2L), taste and adjust.

Fully unlocked ✅ Cook spaghetti just under al dente and serve.
